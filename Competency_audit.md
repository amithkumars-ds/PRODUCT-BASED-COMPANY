## Final Verdict

Your primary problem is not a lack of exposure to technologies.

It is the gap between exposure and independent capability.

The most effective next step is to strengthen Python implementation and software engineering fundamentals, then build one flagship ML system that you can independently implement, test, deploy, and defend.

Your target should be to become an engineer who can reason, implement, debug, and explain—not merely an engineer who knows which tools were used in a project.
